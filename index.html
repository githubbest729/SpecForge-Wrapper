How SpecForge works

  1. Stage. Paste the candidate's CV and the Job Description, then select your output format.
  2. Generate & Handoff. Click Generate Master Prompt, then use the Copy & Open buttons to send the prompt to Claude or ChatGPT.
  3. Format & Print. Paste the AI's final response into the Brief & PDF tab to format it for A4 printing, then save it as a PDF.

Everything stays in your browser and works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste the prompt into an AI tool yourself.
